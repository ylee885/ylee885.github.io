Why do people differ in how they perceive and decide?

The world is in the eye of the beholder. My research examines how cognitive abilities, stimulus properties, and context subjectively shape perception and decision-making. I combine behavioral experiments, computational modeling, and neural and physiological measures to understand how these factors contribute to differences between people.

Methods & toolsBehavioral experiments · Structural equation modeling · Drift-diffusion modeling · fMRI · EEG · Eye tracking · MATLAB · Python · R

Research areas

Decision-Making & Individual Differences

How do people trade speed for accuracy, and why do they often fall short of the best achievable performance? I study suboptimal decisions and how people differ in attention control, working memory, and fluid intelligence.

Neural & Physiological Bases of Cognition

What are the neural and physiological substrates of cognition? My work spans resting-state EEG, fMRI representations of emotion, and pupillometry.
